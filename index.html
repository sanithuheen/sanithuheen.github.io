About

I am an undergraduate student at Brown University studying Computer Engineering with a focus on 3D computer vision, machine learning, and biomedical applications. I am a research student in the Interactive 3D Vision & Learning Lab (IVL), Brown Visual Computing Lab, and the Laboratory for Engineering Man/Machine Systems (LEMS).

My research interests span computer vision, 3D reconstruction, AI/ML systems, medical device innovation, and human-computer interaction. I'm passionate about translating research insights into real-world applications that improve healthcare and advance robotic perception.

Research

3D Reconstruction from Multi-view and Sensor Fusion
08/2025 – Current
Laboratory for Engineering Man/Machine Systems (LEMS), Brown University
Mentors: Prof. Benjamin Kimia, Chiang-Heng Chien (Ph.D. Candidate)
  • Studying theoretical, algorithmic, and engineering foundations of 3D structure reconstruction from multiple 2D images.
  • Implementing camera calibration to relate 3D world coordinates to 2D image coordinates.
  • Applying epipolar geometry and computing essential/fundamental matrices for multi-view geometry.
  • Estimating relative camera poses and performing triangulation for 3D point recovery.
  • Leveraging optimization techniques to improve reconstruction accuracy and robustness.
Markerless Skeletal Kinematic Tracking using 3D Slicer
05/2025 – Current
Biomedical Engineering & Warren Alpert Medical School, Brown University
Mentor: Prof. J.J. Trey Crisco III
  • Extended and validated open-source 3D Slicer tools (SlicerAutoscoperM, Hierarchical 3D Registration) for markerless in vivo skeletal kinematic tracking from biplane videoradiography.
  • Assessed tracking accuracy across multiple skeletal structures and joint implants using Bland-Altman statistical analysis benchmarked against marker-based methods.
  • Applied multi-view 3D reconstruction and camera calibration principles to biplane radiographic pose estimation workflows.
  • Collaborated with a multi-institutional research consortium (Brown, Mayo Clinic, Kitware) supporting global adoption of open-source musculoskeletal tracking software.
Deformable 3D Reconstruction and Tactile Perception in Manipulation
08/2025 – 01/2026
Interactive 3D Vision & Learning Lab (IVL), Brown University
Mentors: Prof. Srinath Sridhar, Research Assistant Angela Xing
  • Collected visuotactile dataset on single-hand and bimanual manipulation of deformable objects using the Brown Interaction Capture System (BrICS).

High-Resolution Esophageal Manometry Redesign
09/2025 – Current
Brown University & RISD Collaborative Design Project
Advisors: Dr. Celinda Kofron (Academic), Dr. Wai-Kit Lo, M.D (Clinical)

Co-leading a year-long capstone effort to redesign High-Resolution Esophageal Manometry (HRM) into a patient-friendly, clinically robust medical device. The project encompasses:

  • Sensing Innovation: Novel sensor form factors and biocompatible materials for improved patient comfort.
  • Electronics Design: Embedded systems and signal processing for real-time pressure data acquisition.
  • Software Development: Clinician-centric interface for data visualization and analysis.
  • Validation & Manufacturability: Clinical validation against industry standards and design for manufacturability.
  • IP Strategy: Patent planning and hospital integration pathways.
